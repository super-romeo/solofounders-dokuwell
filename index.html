How it works

Building a business alone means making every decision without anyone to check it against. There is no co-founder to argue with, no colleague to ask whether a price looks right, and nobody who notices when a week went by without real progress.

Open forums and social networks are full of advice, but most of it comes from people at a very different stage, and much of it is promotion. It is hard to find a few people who are doing the same kind of work right now and will tell you honestly what they see.

The community is built for that. It is a members-only space for solo founders: people who run a product, a service or a small online business on their own, whether full-time or alongside a job.

When you join, you describe where you are: still looking for an idea, building the first version, looking for first customers, or growing an existing business. You are placed in a small group of six to eight founders at a similar stage.

Each group works in writing, so time zones do not matter. Every Monday each member writes one goal for the week. Every Friday they report what happened. Members who are stuck can ask the group for help, and the group sees who has gone quiet.

Beyond the group there is a members-only forum for questions that need more eyes: how to price a first plan, where to find the first customers, how to handle a refund request, what to put on a landing page. Promotion and affiliate links are not allowed, so answers stay useful.

Members can also write short reports of their experiments: what they tried, what it cost in time and money, and what came of it. Over time these reports become a searchable library of real attempts, not success stories.

Groups are reviewed every few months. If your business moves to a new stage, you can move to a group of founders who are there too.

Membership is paid, which keeps the community small and focused on people who are serious about their own business. You can leave at any time.

The community is meant for solo founders anywhere in the world who write in English. It is in preparation: we are checking whether founders need it before it opens.

The community connects founders with each other. It is not a course, an investor network or business advice; decisions about your business stay with you.
